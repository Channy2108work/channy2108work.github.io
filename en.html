Lobster Bay

Seafood restaurant. Building the brand's TikTok channel.

30videos
3shooting days
55,000followers after 3 months
17,000likes after 3 months

Overview

Led the project to build and grow Lobster Bay's TikTok channel, aiming to raise brand awareness.

Challenge

The brand wanted its own staff as actors, people who had never acted in front of a camera.

Solution

  • Met the cast with the director beforehand, then planned a shoot that suited them.
  • Anticipated risks, discussed them with the client and got the plan approved.
  • Coached the cast through each scene on set to keep the quality and emotion of the videos.

Scope of work

  • Drafted and signed the contract between both parties.
  • Worked directly with the client and internal team, from ideas to production.
  • Weekly and monthly reports on key metrics.

Result

With appetizing seafood content, the TikTok channel made a clear impression on Lobster Bay's customers, reaching 55,000 followers and 17,000 likes in just 3 months.
